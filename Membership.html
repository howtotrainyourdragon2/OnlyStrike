Good to know

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Memberships renew automatically but can be cancelled from your account before the next billing date — no lock-in contracts.

Do perks stack with promotions?

Member discounts apply on top of standard rates, but can't be combined with other percentage-off promotions on the same booking.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely — upgrade or downgrade whenever you like. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.
